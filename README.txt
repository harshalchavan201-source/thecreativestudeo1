THE CREATIVE STUDIO — WEBSITE FILES

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the creative_studio_portfolio folder.
3. Double-click index.html. It should open in your browser.

HOW TO EDIT
- index.html: text, sections, project names, contact email.
- style.css: colours, fonts, spacing and layout.
- script.js: menu, project filters and contact form behavior.
- assets/: your logo, certificates and 8 supplied video files.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMIZATION
1. In index.html, replace hello@thecreativestudio.example with your real contact email in BOTH the visible email link and the contact form's recipient in script.js.
2. The eight supplied video clips are included. Their titles are generic placeholders (Creative Edit 01, etc.); edit index.html if you want to replace them with your exact project names.
3. The contact form opens the visitor's email app; it does not store submissions on a server.
4. Google Fonts need an internet connection. The site itself can still open offline using fallback fonts.
5. This is a static website. To publish it, upload the extracted folder to a static hosting service.

The visual direction follows the supplied Creator Aadil reference: dark background, warm gold accents, editorial typography, hero, services, portfolio, credentials and contact sections. The page uses your supplied Creative Studio logo, certificates and video files.
